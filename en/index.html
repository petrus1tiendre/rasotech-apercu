07Common questions

What we are often asked

Where can I get a mechanical part machined in Ouagadougou?

RASOTECH SARL machines mechanical parts in its workshop in Goundry, Rue 28.295, Ouagadougou. The workshop uses its own machines: lathes, milling machines, a pillar drill and a shear. It handles turning, milling, threading, cutting and bending, from one-off parts to batch runs.

Can a broken or worn part be remade without a drawing?

Yes, RASOTECH makes an exact replica of a worn, broken or unobtainable part, even without a drawing. Bring the original part to the workshop in Ouagadougou, or first send a photo of it on WhatsApp to (+226) 70 26 31 21, with its dimensions if you have them. If you have a drawing, the workshop makes the part from that drawing.

How do I request a quote from RASOTECH?

To request a quote from RASOTECH, send a photo of the part on WhatsApp to (+226) 70 26 31 21, with its dimensions if you have them. You can also fill in the quote form at www.rasotech.net, call the office on (+226) 25 36 66 66 or email info@rasotech.net. State the part, the quantity, the material and the deadline you need.

What should I send on WhatsApp to have a part made or repaired?

Send a clear photo of the part, its dimensions if you know them, the quantity, the material and the deadline you need. If you have a drawing, attach it. The WhatsApp number for RASOTECH, a mechanical fabrication workshop in Ouagadougou, is (+226) 70 26 31 21. The workshop replies with a solution and a price.

Which sectors does RASOTECH work for in Burkina Faso?

RASOTECH works primarily for industry in Burkina Faso: mines, cement plants and factories that need to replace a worn, broken or unobtainable part, or to have a custom part made. The Ouagadougou workshop also serves farms, property owners who want an automatic gate or a metal structure, and technicians who need training.

Does RASOTECH make parts for drilling, pumps and mines?

Yes, RASOTECH makes hydraulic components for drilling, water pumps and mining applications. The Ouagadougou workshop has, for example, produced threaded drilling components and a water pump impeller. It also maintains and repairs industrial installations.

What services does RASOTECH offer besides machining?

Besides machining and custom parts, RASOTECH in Ouagadougou offers industrial maintenance, hydraulic equipment for drilling, water pumps and mines, metal structures, agricultural equipment, gate automation, technical training in machining and mechanics, and the sale of tools, equipment and industrial supplies.

Who can automate a gate or build a metal structure in Ouagadougou?

RASOTECH, in Ouagadougou, installs and maintains automatic gates, and fabricates and installs metal structures for construction projects. The workshop has, for example, built a sliding metal gate. To describe your project, send a WhatsApp message to (+226) 70 26 31 21 or use the quote form at www.rasotech.net.

Where is the RASOTECH workshop?

The RASOTECH workshop is in Goundry, Rue 28.295, Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso. Its postal address is 01 BP 4363, Ouagadougou 01. Its GPS coordinates are 12.3800964, -1.4523167. For directions, call the office on (+226) 25 36 66 66 or the mobile on (+226) 76 04 64 64.

What are RASOTECH's opening hours?

RASOTECH is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 17:00, and Saturday from 8:00 to 12:00. The Ouagadougou workshop is closed on Sunday. During these hours, you can call the office on (+226) 25 36 66 66. You can also send a WhatsApp message to (+226) 70 26 31 21 or email info@rasotech.net.
